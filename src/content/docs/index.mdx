## What you build

One <Tooltip term="nuc6cay" label="NUC" /> runs <Tooltip term="nucleus" label="Nucleus" />.
Up to three <Tooltip term="esp32-s3" label="ESP32-S3" /> boards are zone{" "}
<Tooltip term="client-device" label="client devices" />. Press **BOOT** → green
RGB on that zone. Chip temperature streams every 10 s. AWS archives it, tracks
state, alarms on heat, commands the RGB blue from the cloud, and trains a model
the core runs locally (red RGB).

No Terraform. No provision wrappers. Visible <Tooltip term="aws-cli" label="AWS CLI" />
and JSON under `artifacts/`.
